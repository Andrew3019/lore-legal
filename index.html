Lore Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Lore ("Lore", "we", "us", or "our") is a personal journaling application that helps you create written journal entries from your photos and your spoken or typed answers, with the assistance of artificial intelligence. This Privacy Policy describes the categories of information that Lore handles, how that information is used, the third-party service providers that process it on our behalf, how long it is kept, and the choices available to you.

Lore stores your journal locally on your device and in your personal iCloud account. When you use Lore's AI features, portions of your journal content are transmitted to Lore and to its service providers so that the requested feature can be provided, and Lore retains text logs of these interactions for the periods described in Section 6. This policy describes how Lore handles your information.

2. Information We Collect

Lore collects the following categories of information when you use its features:

  • Journal content. When you create an entry, Lore sends the content needed to complete the requested operation. This includes the photo you choose to interview about, any voice recording of your spoken answers, the interview transcript, and the resulting entry text. Photos and audio are processed only to service the request and are not stored on Lore's servers. Text logs of your AI requests and responses, which may include your transcript, your entry text, and any name you have provided, are stored as described in Section 6 (Data Retention) and are used to provide, operate, and improve the app and the quality of its AI responses.
  • Location information. When a photo you interview about includes location metadata, Lore's app converts that metadata on your device into a general place name, such as a city or region (for example, "San Francisco"). Lore may include that general place name in an interview request so that the questions can relate to where the photo was taken. Lore does not receive your device's GPS coordinates. A per-photo location displayed on the in-app map remains on your device and in your personal iCloud account and is not transmitted to Lore's servers or to any service provider.
  • Account and device identifiers. Sign in with Apple provides Lore with a unique, app-specific account identifier that is used to secure access to Lore's backend service. Lore also generates a random identifier on your device that is used to group your activity within our analytics, together with an optional display name if you choose to provide one. These identifiers are not used to identify you outside of Lore.
  • Usage information. Lore collects information about how you interact with the app, such as which screens you open and which features you use, in order to understand and improve the product.
  • Diagnostic information. If the app encounters an error or crash, Lore sends a diagnostic report, which includes technical details such as a stack trace, to our analytics provider so that we can identify and fix problems. These reports do not include your journal content.
  • Technical information. When your device communicates with Lore's backend service or with our analytics provider, technical information is processed as part of that communication, such as your IP address, device model, operating system version, and app version. Our analytics provider may derive an approximate, coarse location (for example, at the level of a city or region) from your IP address. This is standard analytics infrastructure and is not derived from your device's precise location.
  • Operational information. For each AI operation, Lore records technical metadata such as the model used, processing time, token counts, error status, and estimated cost, which Lore uses to operate, secure, and improve the service and the quality of its AI responses.

3. How We Use Your Information

Lore uses the information described above in order to:

  • provide the app's core features, including generating interview questions, transcribing your voice answers, and writing your journal entries;
  • generate optional visual content, such as stickers, when you enable that feature;
  • operate, maintain, and secure the service;
  • monitor and improve the quality of Lore's AI responses and the app as a whole;
  • diagnose errors and crashes; and
  • comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests.

Lore does not sell your personal information. Lore does not use your personal information for advertising, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Service Providers

Lore engages the third-party service providers listed below to process information on its behalf. These providers may process information to provide their services to Lore, to maintain security, to prevent abuse, to comply with law, and for other purposes permitted by their agreements with Lore. Lore requires its service providers to protect your information in a manner consistent with applicable law and this policy.

  • OpenAI. Processes your photos, audio, transcripts, and entry text in order to provide Lore's AI features. OpenAI states that data submitted through its application programming interface is not used to train its models unless the customer explicitly opts in. OpenAI may retain API data for up to 30 days by default for abuse monitoring, subject to limited exceptions described in its data-retention policies, and its retention may also depend on the specific API feature and configuration used.
  • Google. When the optional and experimental generated-stickers feature is enabled, Lore sends image data derived from your photos, together with text prompts, to Google's Gemini models through Google's generative-language API in order to create sticker images. Google's processing and retention of that data are governed by the terms and data policies applicable to that API. The sticker images returned to Lore are stored temporarily as described in Section 6 and are then deleted. This feature is off by default.
  • PostHog. Our product-analytics provider. PostHog receives usage and diagnostic information, your device identifier, your display name if you provided one, and technical information such as your IP address, device model, operating system version, and app version. PostHog also receives text copies of the requests submitted to and the responses returned by Lore's AI features, which Lore uses to monitor and improve the quality of its AI responses. These text copies may contain personal or sensitive information that you included in your transcripts or journal entries. PostHog does not receive your photo files or your audio files. Lore does not enable session recording, and PostHog does not capture screenshots or recordings of your screen.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Precise location. Lore does not receive your device's GPS coordinates. As described in Section 2, a photo's location metadata is converted on your device into a general place name before anything is sent to Lore. (Separately, as described in Section 2 and Section 4, our analytics provider may derive a coarse, city-level location from your IP address as part of standard analytics infrastructure.)
  • Your complete journal library. Lore does not maintain a server-side copy of your complete journal library. Your photos, transcripts, and entries are stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account. Text included in individual AI requests and responses may be stored as described in Sections 2, 4, and 6.
  • Contact details. Aside from the account identifier provided by Sign in with Apple, Lore does not collect contact information such as your email address, telephone number, or mailing address. Lore does not request your name or email address from Apple during sign in.

6. Data Retention

  • Text logs of your AI requests and responses, which may include your transcript, entry text, and any name you provided, together with the account and device identifiers and the operational metadata associated with those requests, are retained on Lore's servers for up to 365 days, measured from the date of each interaction, after which an automated process deletes them.
  • Generated sticker images, when created, are stored on Lore's servers for up to 7 days and are then automatically deleted.
  • The information sent to PostHog, including the text copies of AI requests and responses, is retained by PostHog for up to approximately 30 days.
  • Data processed by OpenAI is retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days by default, subject to the exceptions and configuration described in Section 4.
  • Information stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account is retained until you delete it.

Provider processing and deletion may be carried out asynchronously and may take a short time to complete.

7. Data Deletion and Your Choices

You may delete your data at any time by opening the app and selecting Settings, then Delete all my data. When you do so, Lore initiates deletion of the information it holds on its servers, including your AI request and response logs and any generated sticker images, initiates deletion of your associated analytics information held by PostHog, initiates revocation of Lore's Sign in with Apple authorization, and erases the app's local data on your device, which then propagates to your personal iCloud account.

Some of these deletions, particularly those carried out by our service providers, are asynchronous and may not occur immediately. If an automated deletion cannot be completed at the time, Lore will complete or investigate the request through its support process. You may contact us using the details in Section 12 for confirmation or assistance.

Photos already saved to your device's system photo library, and any copies held in your own iCloud backups, are controlled by you and are not deleted by this request.

8. Children's Privacy

Lore is not directed to children under the age of 13, and Lore does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Lore, please contact us using the details in Section 12 so that we can take appropriate action.

9. Security

Lore transmits information to its backend service and service providers over encrypted connections, and limits the information it transmits to what is needed to provide the requested features. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and Lore cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Users

Lore is operated from the United States, and the information described in this policy is processed in the United States and in the locations used by the service providers listed in Section 4. If you use Lore from outside the United States, you understand that your information is processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your location.

11. Changes to This Policy

Lore may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes to the information we collect or to the retention periods described here, we will update this policy and revise the effective date shown above.
