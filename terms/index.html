Terms of use

Last updated: November 3, 2026

These terms cover Lore Friends, which lets you share entries you choose with a small circle of friends. They apply to your friends profile (display name, username, and photo), to every entry you share, to every comment you leave, and to every sticker you place on a friend's photo. Your private journal is not covered by them: nothing in it is shared unless you choose Share with friends for that entry.

What is and is not allowed on Friends is in the Community rules. How Lore stores, keeps, and deletes your information is in the Privacy policy.

Who can use Friends

  • You must be 13 or older.
  • You sign in with Apple and have one friends profile. Friends are people who accepted your invite or friend request, or whose invite or friend request you accepted. You can send a friend request from someone's profile or from search.
  • People can find you by your @username unless you turn off Let people find me by username. A People you may know list shows friends of your friends who have that switch on. If you add your phone number, people who have it in their contacts can see that you are on Lore. There is no public profile and no public feed.
  • Each person can have up to 25 friends.

Your content and Lore

  • You keep ownership of what you share. By sharing an entry or setting a profile name or photo, you let Lore store it, process it (including the automated moderation check), and show it to your current friends, only to run Friends. The same applies to your comments, shown as described in the Community rules. That permission ends when you unshare the entry, delete your friends profile, or delete your account, apart from the backup and retention periods described in the Privacy policy.
  • You are responsible for what you share and must have the right to share it.
  • Lore may change or stop offering Friends. Turning Friends off does not delete your private journal. How Lore stores, keeps, and deletes friends data is described in the Privacy policy.

Suspension

For serious or repeated violations of the Community rules, Lore may suspend your friends access. While suspended, you cannot use Friends and your profile and shared entries are hidden from everyone, but your private journal is unaffected, and you can still delete your friends profile or your whole account.

Deleting your friends data

  • Delete your friends profile from the friends settings to remove your profile, photo, username, phone number if you added one, invite code, friends, friend requests, blocks, shared entries and their discussion, your comments, likes, stickers, and tags, and the reports you filed, while keeping your private journal and account.
  • Delete my account and data in Settings removes all of that and your account.
