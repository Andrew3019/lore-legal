Support

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Get help

For questions, problems with the app, requests about your information, safety concerns, or a moderation decision you think was a mistake, email nelsonlou04@gmail.com. A person will read your message.

Delete your account

In Lore, open Settings, then choose Delete my account and data. This deletes your account, the information Lore holds on its servers, and your journal on your device and in your iCloud. What is deleted, and what is not, is described in the Privacy policy.

To leave Friends but keep your journal and account, delete your friends profile from the friends settings instead.

Report or block someone in Friends

  • Report a shared entry, a comment, or a sticker that breaks the Community rules by choosing Report entry, Report comment, or Report sticker on it. Choose a reason and, if you like, add a short note. The person you report is not told who reported them.
  • Remove a friend to end the friendship, or Block someone to remove them and stop either of you from reaching the other in Friends.

Safety concerns

If something in Friends puts you or someone else at risk, report it in the app as above so Lore can review it, and email nelsonlou04@gmail.com if you need to tell us more.

Lore is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number.
