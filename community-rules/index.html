Community rules

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Lore Friends lets you share entries you choose with a small circle of friends. These rules explain what belongs there, what does not, and what Lore does when something goes wrong. They apply to your friends profile (display name, username, and photo), to every entry you share, to every comment you leave, and to every sticker you place on a friend's photo. Your private journal is not covered by them: nothing in it is shared unless you choose Share with friends for that entry.

Who can use Friends, and the terms that come with it, are in the Terms of use. How Lore handles your information is in the Privacy policy.

What you share

  • An entry stays private until you share it. A shared entry is a copy of its title, text, highlights, date and time, general place label, song details, and up to five still images. The transcript, precise location, people you tagged in your private journal, emotion and personality readings, and video are never shared.
  • When you share an entry, you can choose to tag up to 10 of your friends on it. A person you tag can remove their own tag.
  • Everything you share is visible to all of your current friends, including entries you shared before they became your friend. There are no view counts and no list of who viewed it.
  • You can edit a shared entry, unshare it, or delete it at any time. Unsharing removes it from Friends immediately.
  • Share only what is yours to share. Do not share another person's private information, or photos of them, where they would not want it shared.

Comments, likes, and stickers

  • You can comment on any shared entry you can see, including your own, and you can reply to a comment. A comment or reply is up to 500 characters of text and may carry one of your own stickers or one photo. Comments cannot be edited; delete one and write it again.
  • You can like a shared entry you can see, once.
  • You can place one of your own stickers on a photo in a friend's shared entry, one sticker per person per photo. You can move, replace, or remove your own sticker, and the entry's author can remove any sticker on their entry.
  • Who sees them: the entry's author sees every comment on it. Everyone else sees only their own and those of the author and of their own current friends. If you and another of the author's friends are not friends with each other, neither of you sees the other's name or comment there. Likes and stickers follow the same rule. A block in either direction hides both of you from each other.
  • You can delete your own comments at any time. The author of an entry can delete any comment on it. Deleting a comment also deletes the replies under it.
  • Unsharing or deleting an entry deletes its comments, likes, stickers, and tags; sharing it again starts fresh. Ending a friendship or blocking deletes both people's comments, likes, stickers, and tags on each other's entries, and re-friending does not bring them back.
  • You may get a notification when a friend comments on, likes, or places a sticker on your entry, replies to your comment, or tags you in an entry, and when someone sends you a friend request. The notification shows a name, never the comment itself. Lore keeps no record of who read anything.

What is not allowed

Lore allows ordinary profanity and honest discussion of hard subjects, including sex, violence, discrimination, slurs, trauma, and self-harm, when you are talking about your own life or experience. What is not allowed, in a display name, username, profile photo, shared entry, comment, or sticker:

  • sexual content involving minors, in any form;
  • explicit pornography;
  • graphic gore;
  • credible threats of violence against a person or group;
  • severe targeted harassment, or hateful threats against people because of who they are;
  • instructions or encouragement for suicide or self-harm.

Lore checks every display name, username, profile photo, comment (with its sticker or photo), sticker you place on a friend's photo, and photo in an entry you try to share with an automated moderation service before it becomes visible. A shared entry's photos are refused only for nudity, sexual content involving minors, or graphic gore; its words are not checked automatically, since only your friends can see it, but these rules still apply and friends can report it. If the check rejects something, nothing is shared and your private entry is unchanged.

Reporting, blocking, and removing friends

  • Report a shared entry, a comment, or a sticker on a photo that you can see if it breaks these rules; the author of an entry can also report a comment or sticker on it. Choose a reason and, if you like, add a short note. The person you report is not told who reported them, and reporting does not hide the entry, comment, or sticker by itself.
  • Remove a friend to end the friendship. Neither of you sees the other's shared entries after that.
  • Block a friend, or someone you can see but are not friends with, to remove them and stop either of you from viewing or accepting the other's invite link or friend request, or finding the other in search. Unblocking does not restore the friendship.

What Lore does about reports

  • Lore reviews reports by hand at least once each business day. There is no automated banning.
  • If a shared entry, comment, or sticker breaks these rules, Lore may remove it. A removed entry cannot be restored, though you may share the entry again once it follows the rules.
  • For serious or repeated violations, Lore may suspend your friends access, as described in the Terms of use.
  • Lore may report content that suggests a child is being exploited, or that someone is in immediate danger, to the appropriate authorities where the law requires or allows it.
  • If you think Lore made a mistake, contact us at the address below. There is no formal appeals process yet; a person will read your message.

Lore is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number.
